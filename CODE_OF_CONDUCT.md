# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want participation in Rahasyakosh to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient and constructive; assume good faith.
- Give and accept critical feedback gracefully; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, doxxing, or publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

Report violations privately by contacting the maintainer through GitHub (https://github.com/enhariharan). All reports
will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
